# Code of Conduct

Earshot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in this project's issues, pull requests and other spaces agrees to it.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully. Focus on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language, or publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone behaves unacceptably, use **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request (the `…` menu) and choose **Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainer sees these reports. To report a problem that isn't tied to one comment, open an issue and ask the maintainer to contact you. Leave out the details.

The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity private, and may edit or remove content, or block anyone who breaks this code. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
